A good gift does not need to be expensive. It needs to show that you thought about the person receiving it.

## Our five favorites

1. A gift voucher for an experience they would not book for themselves
2. Something handmade, with a short note on how it was made
3. A small bundle of everyday things they love
4. A subscription that arrives every month
5. Time together, planned in advance
