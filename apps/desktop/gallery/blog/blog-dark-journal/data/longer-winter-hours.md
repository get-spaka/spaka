Many of you told us that six o'clock came too early, especially in the darker months. From **1 December**, we will stay open until 8 pm from Monday to Friday.

## What changes

Saturday hours stay the same, from 9 am to 5 pm, and we remain closed on Sundays. Bookings for the evening slots open next week.

Thank you for all your feedback. If there is anything else that would make your visits easier, [let us know](/contact).
