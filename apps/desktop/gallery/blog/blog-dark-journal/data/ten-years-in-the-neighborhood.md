Ten years ago, we opened with one room, two people and a lot of hope. Today we are a team of six, and many of our first customers still visit every month.

## What has not changed

We still know most of you by name, we still answer the phone ourselves, and we still believe that good work takes the time it takes.

Thank you for ten wonderful years. Here is to the next ten.
