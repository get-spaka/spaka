Our day starts at seven, well before the first customer arrives. The first hour is for checking orders, preparing materials and making sure everything is ready.

## Small routines that matter

- A quick team catch-up over coffee
- A look at the day's bookings and deliveries
- Ten minutes to tidy and set up the front of the shop

These small habits keep the rest of the day calm, even when it gets busy.
